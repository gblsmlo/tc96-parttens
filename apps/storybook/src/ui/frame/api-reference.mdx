# Frame

A framed container that groups related information on a tinted surface: the members of a workspace, a billing history table, the plan and the card of a billing page. It holds a header with a title and a description, one or more panels with the content and a footer, and the header and footer sit on the frame around the panels.

The other pages of `UI/Frame`: [Playground](?path=/story/ui-frame-playground--playground) to try the texts, the parts and a second panel, [Stories](?path=/story/ui-frame-stories--default) for the base frame, [Behavior](?path=/story/ui-frame-behavior--part-elements) for the tests, [Usages](?path=/story/ui-frame-usages--team-members) for real content and [Layouts](?path=/story/ui-frame-layouts--separated-panels) for how the parts combine.

## Import

```tsx
import {
  Frame,
  FrameDescription,
  FrameFooter,
  FrameHeader,
  FramePanel,
  FrameTitle,
} from '@acme/ui/frame'
```

## Anatomy

<Canvas of={ApiReferenceStories.Anatomy} />

```tsx
<Frame>
  <FrameHeader>
    <FrameTitle />
    <FrameDescription />
  </FrameHeader>
  <FramePanel />
  <FrameFooter />
</Frame>
```

Every part is optional inside `Frame`, and none of them reads context, so they render anywhere. The parts have no props of their own: each takes the props of its element, and `className` adds classes after the part's own, so a conflicting class wins (`p-0` on a `FramePanel` removes its padding and keeps its border and radius).

## Frame

The tinted surface that holds the other parts. Renders a `div` with no role, laid out as a flex column, with `rounded-2xl`, the `muted` background at 72% opacity and 4px of padding (`p-1`).

Also takes every prop of a `div`.

| Attribute | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-slot` | `"frame"` | Selects the part in the theme and from other parts |

A `FramePanel` that is a direct child of `Frame` and comes right after another `FramePanel` gets a 4px top margin (`mt-1`). The rule matches `data-slot="frame-panel"` siblings only: a panel after the header, a panel wrapped in another element and a footer after a panel get no margin.

A `Table` with `variant="card"` can take the place of a panel: the table's card variant is styled to sit on the frame surface.

## FrameHeader

The top of the frame, outside the panels. Renders a `header`, laid out as a flex column with 20px of horizontal and 16px of vertical padding (`px-5 py-4`) and no gap between the title and the description.

Also takes every prop of a `header`.

| Attribute | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-slot` | `"frame-panel-header"` | Selects the part in the theme and from other parts |

## FrameTitle

The title of the frame. Renders a `div` in `font-semibold` and `text-sm`, which is not a heading. To make it one, put a heading element inside it (`<FrameTitle><h2>Invoices</h2></FrameTitle>`); under Tailwind's preflight the heading inherits the title's size and weight.

Also takes every prop of a `div`.

| Attribute | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-slot` | `"frame-panel-title"` | Selects the part in the theme and from other parts |

## FrameDescription

The line under the title. Renders a `div` in `text-muted-foreground` and `text-sm`.

Also takes every prop of a `div`.

| Attribute | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-slot` | `"frame-panel-description"` | Selects the part in the theme and from other parts |

## FramePanel

A surface for the content. Renders a `div` with `rounded-xl`, a 1px `border`, the `background` color, 20px of padding (`p-5`) and the theme's card shadow (`shadow-(shadow:--card-shadow)`, the `--card-shadow` token).

Also takes every prop of a `div`.

| Attribute | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-slot` | `"frame-panel"` | Selects the part in the theme and from other parts |

A `::before` that covers the panel, with no pointer events and a radius of `--radius-xl` minus 1px, casts a 1px shadow line: along the bottom border in black at 4% in light, along the top border in white at 6% in dark.

A list or a table that runs edge to edge takes `className="p-0"` on the panel and pads its own rows.

## FrameFooter

The bottom of the frame, outside the panels. Renders a `footer` with 20px of horizontal and 16px of vertical padding (`px-5 py-4`) and no layout of its own: add `flex` and a justify class to place a note beside a button.

Also takes every prop of a `footer`.

| Attribute | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-slot` | `"frame-panel-footer"` | Selects the part in the theme and from other parts |

## Accessibility and theme

- `Frame` has no role and no name. A frame that is a section of the page goes inside a `section` with `aria-labelledby` pointing at the heading in its `FrameTitle`, which makes it a named region.
- `FrameHeader` and `FrameFooter` are `header` and `footer` elements. Outside an `article`, `aside`, `main`, `nav` or `section` they map to the page's `banner` and `contentinfo` landmarks, so place a frame inside `main` or a `section`.
- The header and the footer sit on the `muted` surface at 72% opacity, and the panels on `background`. Muted text in the header and footer (`FrameDescription`, a footer note) needs `muted-foreground` to reach 4.5:1 over that surface in the theme.
- The radii come from the theme's `--radius-2xl` and `--radius-xl`. The panel border has no color class: its color comes from the theme's base border rule (`border-border` in the example theme).
